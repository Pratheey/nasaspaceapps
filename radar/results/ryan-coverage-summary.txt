We compared NISAR and Sentinel-1 over the same 30 Corcoran-area fields using 23 of 23 planned pairs with equal durations and start dates at most six days apart. At the 0.30 median coherence threshold, NISAR passed 542 of 690 field checks (78.6%), and Sentinel-1 passed 558 of 690 field checks (80.9%). The result compares the available products over these fields and dates. Radar wavelength, imaging geometry, processing and the date offsets can affect coherence.
