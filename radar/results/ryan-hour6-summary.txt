We compared NISAR HH and HV backscatter over 30 fields near Corcoran across 26 dates and obtained 780 usable paired field measurements from 780 possible. The median across dates of each group's field-median HV/HH ratio was alfalfa -7.7 dB, almonds -6.1 dB, cotton -7.9 dB. The groups use 2025 USDA crop labels, and differences in the radar echo can reflect crop structure, moisture, and field management.
